# Mission Approval Implementation Plan

> Run with /implement-plan /Users/janeshorst/Dev/mutinex-interview/.work/mission-approval/plan.md in a fresh context.

**Goal:** Add submit, withdraw, approve and reject for missions in the API and the CLI. A director who does not lead the mission approves or rejects it, and sees the matcher preview in `mc missions show`.

**Architecture:** The missions router gets one transition table `action -> (from, to)` and four POST routes. Each route locks the mission, checks the actor, checks the status and sets the new status. The step 5 match route loads its data inside the handler. This plan moves that load into one function, `build_preview`, which the match route and `mission_out` both call. `MissionOut` adds the approver and the preview. The CLI adds four commands, and `print_mission` prints the approver and the preview table.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, FastAPI, Pydantic 2, SQLAlchemy 2, Typer, httpx, Rich, pytest, Postgres.

**Spec:** /Users/janeshorst/Dev/mutinex-interview/.work/mission-approval/spec.md

**Contract:** Covers, Tests must prove, Files, Interfaces, Global Constraints and Checkpoint are binding. Report each change to them to the human. The implementer chooses the code.

# Spec: mission-approval

## Goal
Add the mission approval of build step 6 (`design/DESIGN.md:69`) to the API and the CLI. The lead submits and withdraws a mission. A director who does not lead the mission approves or rejects it, and sees the matcher preview in the mission view (DESIGN req 8, 9).

## Acceptance criteria
| ID | Criterion | The tests must prove |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | The mission lead submits a DRAFT mission (DESIGN 251). | `POST /missions/{id}/submit` by the mission lead gives 200 and a `MissionOut` with status `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`. This is true for a lead and for a director who leads the mission. A mission with no slot can be submitted. |
| AC2 | The mission lead withdraws a mission from APPROVAL_REQUIRED (DESIGN 252). | `POST /missions/{id}/withdraw` by the mission lead gives 200 and status `DRAFT`. `approved_by_username` and `approved_at` are `null`. After the withdraw, `PATCH /missions/{id}` by the lead gives 200. |
| AC3 | Only the mission lead submits and withdraws (DESIGN 323-324). | Another lead and a director of the own org each get 403 on submit and on withdraw. The body is `{"role": "<ROLE>", "rule": "Directors and leads submit and withdraw only their own missions"}`. The status does not change. |
| AC4 | A director who does not lead the mission approves it (DESIGN 253, 331). | `POST /missions/{id}/approve` by such a director gives 200, status `APPROVED`, `approved_by_username` = the director, and `approved_at` between the time before and after the request. A mission that a director leads is approved by another director of the org. |
| AC5 | A director who does not lead the mission rejects it, without a comment (DESIGN 252, 267). | `POST /missions/{id}/reject` with no body gives 200 and status `DRAFT`. `approved_by_username` and `approved_at` are `null`. After the reject, the lead edits the mission and submits it again with 200. |
| AC6 | A lead never approves or rejects (DESIGN 331). | A lead gets 403 on approve and on reject. The body is `{"role": "LEAD", "rule": "Only directors approve or reject missions"}`. This is true also when the lead leads the mission. The status does not change. |
| AC7 | A mission lead never approves or rejects their own mission. A director-led mission needs another director (DESIGN 339-340). | The director who leads the mission gets 403 on approve and on reject. The body is `{"role": "DIRECTOR", "rule": "A mission lead never approves or rejects their own mission"}`. The status stays `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`. |
| AC8 | Each action is allowed only from one status (DESIGN 250-253). Withdraw from APPROVED is build step 8 (DESIGN 71). | For each action and each other status, the allowed actor gets 409 with `{"detail": "Mission is <STATUS>. <Action> is allowed only in <FROM>."}`. `<Action>` is `Submit`, `Withdraw`, `Approve` or `Reject`. Example: withdraw of an APPROVED mission gives `Mission is APPROVED. Withdraw is allowed only in APPROVAL_REQUIRED.` The status does not change. |
| AC9 | The check order is crew 403, then 404, then 403, then 409. | A lead who does not lead a COMPLETED mission gets 403 on submit, not 409. A lead of org B gets 404 on approve of a DRAFT mission of org A, not 403. |
| AC10 | Crew members have no access to the transitions (DESIGN 331). | A crew member gets 403 on each of the four routes. The body is `{"role": "CREW", "rule": "Only directors and leads manage and view missions"}`. |
| AC11 | A mission of another org is not visible (DESIGN req 4, 22). | A director and a lead of org B each get 404 on each of the four routes for a mission of org A. Each body equals the body for a random UUID: `{"detail": "Not found"}`. The org A mission does not change. |
| AC12 | The mission details are final in APPROVAL_REQUIRED (DESIGN 267, 274). | `PATCH /missions/{id}`, a slot add and a slot remove by the lead each give 409 when the mission is `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`. |
| AC13 | `MissionOut` shows who approved the mission (DESIGN 198). | `GET /missions/{id}` of a seeded APPROVED mission gives `approved_by_username` = the seeded approver and the seeded `approved_at`. A DRAFT mission that was never approved gives `null` for both. |
| AC14 | The mission view shows the matcher preview while the mission waits for approval (DESIGN req 9, 267). | `GET /missions/{id}` of an `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` mission gives `preview` = a `MatchPreview`. It equals the body of `GET /missions/{id}/match` for the same mission and data in DRAFT, before the submit. A director who does not lead the mission and a lead who does not lead the mission both get it. In each other status, `preview` is `null`. The view writes no rows. |
| AC15 | The match route keeps its step 5 rules. | In `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`, `GET /missions/{id}/match` gives 409 to the mission lead and 403 to a director who does not lead the mission. All step 5 matcher tests still pass. |
| AC16 | Each transition and each rejected transition writes an info log (CLAUDE.md "Conventions"). | Each 200 transition and each 409 writes one INFO record of the logger `mc_api.routers.missions` with the mission ID. No record contains a token. |
| AC17 | The CLI runs the transitions (DESIGN 426). | `mc missions submit\|withdraw\|approve\|reject <mission-id>` each call the API and print the mission with the new status. A 403, 404, 409 or 422 exits 1 with the API message and no traceback. |
| AC18 | `mc missions show` prints the approver and the preview. | For an approved mission, the output has the line `Approved by <username> at <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM> UTC`. For an `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` mission, the output has the preview table of `mc missions match` (Slot, Skill, Min level, Crew, Level, Workload days, `No match` for an unfilled slot) and the line `<N> of <M> slots filled`. Other statuses print no preview. |
| AC19 | The design document describes the transition table as built. | `design/DESIGN.md:276` says that the code holds the transitions as `action -> (from, to)` and that the route checks the actor. The "Changes" table has a row for this step with the reason: withdraw and reject have the same `(from, to)` and different actors. |

## Non-goals
- Withdraw from APPROVED to DRAFT, start, complete and cancel (build step 8).
- Invites and the "open slots only" rule (build step 7).
- A reject comment or a cancel reason (P2).
- Approval workflow customization or more than one approval (P2, DESIGN 277).
- The matcher route for directors or in APPROVAL_REQUIRED. The approval view is `mc missions show` only.
- A status filter or a flag column in `mc missions list`.
- New behaviour for the additional-director flag. The no-self-approval rule makes sure that another director approves.
- A README change. Build step 11 writes the demo (DESIGN 74).
- A schema change or a migration. `approved_by` and `approved_at` exist.
